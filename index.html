♟ Backgammon

White moves from the top-right to the bottom-right (24 → 1) and bears off to the bottom tray; Black moves the opposite way. Click a highlighted checker, then a highlighted destination. You must use both dice if possible (all four on doubles). Hit a lone checker to send it to the bar; bar checkers must re-enter first. Bear off only when all your checkers are in your home board. Shortcuts: Space roll · U undo.
